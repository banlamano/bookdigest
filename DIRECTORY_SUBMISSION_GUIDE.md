Book Digest is an AI-powered book summary platform designed for busy professionals, entrepreneurs, and lifelong learners who want to extract maximum value from books in minimum time.

**What makes Book Digest different:**

Unlike shallow paragraph summaries, each book on Book Digest includes:
- **Full Summary** - Comprehensive overview of the book's main themes
- **Key Insights** - The most important takeaways and lessons
- **Chapter Breakdowns** - Detailed exploration of each chapter
- **Memorable Quotes** - The best quotes to remember and share
- **Action Items** - Specific steps you can take to apply the ideas

**For whom:**
- Busy professionals who want to read more but lack time
- Entrepreneurs seeking business wisdom from proven books
- Students looking for efficient study resources
- Anyone building a reading habit

**Pricing:**
- Free tier: 3 books per month
- Premium: €4.99/month for unlimited access to 450+ books

**Technology:**
Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and powered by OpenAI for high-quality AI summaries.
