### Content

- ✅ **Action Items** (7-10 per book)
- ✅ **All Book Covers** (Open Library CDN)

### Features
- ✅ **Enhanced Reading Experience** (5 tabs: Summary, Insights, Quotes, Chapters, Actions)
- ✅ **AI Audio Narration** (Text-to-Speech with full controls)
- ✅ **Reading Progress Tracking** (automatic, saves to database)
- ✅ **Bookmarks/Favorites** (instant save)
- ✅ **Reading History** (tracks completed books)
- ✅ **User Authentication** (JWT-based)
- ✅ **Responsive Design** (works on all devices)
- ✅ **Dark Mode** (full support)
- ✅ **PWA Ready** (installable on mobile)

### Tech Stack
- ✅ **Frontend:** Next.js 14 + React + Tailwind CSS (Vercel)
- ✅ **Backend:** Node.js + Express + Prisma (Render)
- ✅ **Database:** PostgreSQL (Neon)
