## 🎯 Next Steps - Submit to Search Engines (DO NOW!)

### **Step 1: Google Search Console** (5 minutes)

1. **Go to:** https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click **"Add Property"**
3. Choose **"URL prefix"**
4. Enter: `https://book-digest.com`
5. Click **"Verify"** (HTML tag already in site!)
6. Go to **"Sitemaps"** → Submit: `sitemap.xml`
7. **Request indexing** for these URLs:
   ```
   https://book-digest.com
   https://book-digest.com/categories
   https://book-digest.com/pricing
   https://book-digest.com/blog
   https://book-digest.com/blog/top-10-business-books-2026
   ```

### **Step 2: Bing Webmaster Tools** (5 minutes)

1. **Go to:** https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Click **"Add a site"**
3. Enter: `https://book-digest.com`
4. **Import from Google Search Console** (easiest!)
5. Submit sitemap: `https://book-digest.com/sitemap.xml`
