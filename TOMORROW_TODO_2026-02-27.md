#### 3. ICP Scoring Exercise (45 mins) ⚡ HIGHEST LEVERAGE
**Framework:** Pain (32pts) + Fit (27pts) + Business (35pts) + Value (21pts) = 115 total

**Score these 6 segments:**

1. **B2B Sales Professionals**
   - Pain: Need conversation starters, credibility with clients
   - Fit: Time-starved, need quick insights
   - Business: Company expense accounts, easy to find on LinkedIn
   - Value: High lifetime value, low support cost

2. **Management Consultants**
   - Pain: Must stay current on business thinking, client conversations
   - Fit: Perfect match - need breadth over depth
   - Business: Expense accounts, fast decision makers
   - Value: High willingness to pay, potential corporate deals

3. **MBA Students/Early-Career Professionals**
   - Pain: Overwhelming reading lists, career positioning
   - Fit: Great fit, cost-sensitive
   - Business: Lower budgets, high volume potential
   - Value: Lower LTV, but viral potential

4. **Tech Leads/CTOs**
   - Pain: Stay current on management/leadership
   - Fit: Good fit, prefer technical depth sometimes
   - Business: Company budgets, medium accessibility
   - Value: Good LTV, low support needs

5. **Content Creators/Newsletter Writers**
   - Pain: Need source material, idea generation
   - Fit: Perfect fit for research
   - Business: Variable budgets, medium to find
   - Value: Medium LTV, could become evangelists

6. **Career Coaches**
   - Pain: Need resources for clients
   - Fit: Good fit, need curated content
   - Business: Small budgets, harder to find at scale
   - Value: Medium LTV, potential partnerships
