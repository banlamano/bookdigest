PRODUCT HUNT FAQ RESPONSES (copy/paste)

1) Is it free?
Yes—there’s a free plan so anyone can try it. Free users can read a limited number of full digests per month. Premium unlocks unlimited access.

2) What do I get with Premium?
Premium unlocks unlimited reading + full digests (insights, chapters, quotes, action items) without monthly limits. We’re also working on audio features (marked as beta/coming soon).

3) How is this different from Blinkist?
Blinkist is great for short summaries. Book Digest focuses on structured output you can act on: key insights, chapter breakdowns, memorable quotes, and action items—designed to turn reading into execution.

4) Are the quotes real quotes from the book?
They’re “memorable quote-style” takeaways generated to capture key ideas. We’re exploring ways to make this even more accurate and transparent over time.

5) How do you create the digests?
We generate structured digests using AI (OpenAI), producing consistent sections (summary → insights → chapters → action plan). Quality is a top priority and we’re iterating quickly based on feedback.

6) Do you have an app?
Right now it’s a web app you can install on mobile (PWA). Native iOS/Android apps are planned.

7) Do you support offline?
Offline reading/audio downloads are planned—currently marked as coming soon. The goal is to make it great on mobile.

8) Where do you get the book list / covers?
Books and covers are pulled from public metadata sources (e.g., Google Books) and curated in the library.

9) What’s next on the roadmap?
Top priorities: real audio narration, offline downloads, and more personalization (saved highlights / exports). Feedback from PH will strongly influence what we build next.

10) Any launch deal?
Yes—use code PH20 for 20% off (Monthly + Yearly).
